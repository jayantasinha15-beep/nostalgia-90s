🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At 90s Nostalgia Machine, we respect your privacy. This privacy document outlines how user data is handled while using our website.

1. Data Collection & Usage

This website operates as a client-side retro web showcase. We do not require account creation, nor do we collect sensitive personal data such as financial information or passwords.

2. Local Storage (Client-Side)

Interactive features such as the Memory Diary / Guestbook may save text entries directly inside your browser's localStorage. This data stays entirely on your own device and is not transmitted to any remote tracking server.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We may use standard web hosting analytics (e.g., page views) to understand performance and site availability. No cross-site intrusive trackers or advertising pixels are injected to harvest personal information.

4. External Links & Media

Audio clips and historical imagery are used strictly under fair-use commentary and nostalgic tribute. External media streams may be served via public CDNs or web archives.
