📼 About The Project

90s Nostalgia Machine holo ekta interactive digital time capsule, jekhane 1990-er doshoker shondhye, Sunday Doordarshan, gully cricket, ar chhotobelar shob authentic anondo-ke abar notun bhabe smoron kora hoyeche.

🎯 Amader Uddessho (Our Mission)

Smartphone ebong high-speed internet-er ager sei shanto, shorol somoygulo—jekhane Phantom candy mukhe die dhowa chharar acting kora hoto, cassette phite jorale Nataraj pencil die ghorano hoto, ar bikel chartey mathay cricket khele fire ghore mombaatir aaloy pora shuru hoto—sei smritigulo digital bhabe bachiye rakha.

🕹️ Interactive Features

  • CRT Television: Channel switch kore DD1, cartoon, o purono games dekhar obhigyota.
  • Walkman Player: Iconic audio themes ar jingles.
  • Load-Shedding Mode: Switch off kore mombaatir aalote shanto shondhye obubhob kora.
  • Khajna Tray: Chhotobelar card o gadgets-er interactive collection.
